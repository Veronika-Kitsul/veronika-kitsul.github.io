**Visiting Researcher, CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security (Summer 2023)**
Hanover, Germany — in Vaibhav Bajpai's research group.

This work studied the global uptake and performance of the DNS-over-QUIC (DoQ) protocol, and was later published in _IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management_:

> Jayasree Sengupta, Mike Kosek, Justus Fries, **Veronika Kitsul**, Vaibhav Bajpai. "A Long-Term View of DNS Over QUIC Adoption and Its Performance Impact on YouTube Streaming." _IEEE TNSM_, 2026. [doi:10.1109/TNSM.2026.3688441](https://doi.org/10.1109/TNSM.2026.3688441)

- Applied computer network security knowledge to study the global uptake and performance of DNS over QUIC.
- Analyzed two years of measurement data using Python to assess resolver adoption trends and protocol-level performance.
- Created all visualizations for the study, including time series plots, adoption breakdowns, and latency comparisons across major DNS resolvers.
