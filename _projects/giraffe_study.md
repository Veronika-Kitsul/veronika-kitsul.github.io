**HackHarvard 2023 (Fall 2023)**

giraffe.study uses AI to quickly create videos that explain a specific topic. Our team, with members from Princeton, Columbia, and Carleton College, won the **"So you think you can do it better?"** track at HackHarvard 2023, out of 635 participants and 150+ projects.

- Built a pipeline that autonomously produces educational videos over 2 minutes long.
- Used Python, Bash scripting, prompt engineering, and LLM APIs.

Code: [github.com/Giraffe-AI/giraffe](https://github.com/Giraffe-AI/giraffe) · Harvard SEAS wrote about the hackathon in [Hacking the future](https://seas.harvard.edu/news/hacking-future).
